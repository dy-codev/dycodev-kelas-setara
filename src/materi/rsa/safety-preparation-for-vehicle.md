# 6 Safety Preparation for Vehicle

Airside driver has responsible to ensure that the vehicle are roadworthy before driving, they should conduct safety preparation i.e.:

- Check that the vehicle permit still valid;

- Check that all equipment on vehicle are work properly (light, brake, etc.);

- Check that there are no leakages on fuel and oil tank;

- Check that there are no leakages from combustion chamber;

- Check that the vehicle equipped with fire extinguisher placed safely, easy to reach and ready to operate ;

- Check that the safety belt are work properly;

- There is flame trap for vehicle with the fuel other than solar (diesel).
