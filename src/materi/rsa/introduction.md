# 1 Introduction

Safety must remain our number one priority on all of aviation activities. Relating to ramp activities, all personnel are required to implement and maintain safety in order to minimize incidents or accidents that can harm both financial and human life. This course is intended to provide ramp personnel with knowledge, skill, and attitudes required to implement and maintain ramp safety in accordance with national regulation and ICAO standard.
